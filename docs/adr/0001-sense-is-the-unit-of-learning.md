# Sense is the unit of learning

Learning Status, Review Step, Usage Verdicts and Targets all attach to a **Sense** (one meaning of a Headword), never to the Headword itself. The PRD asks for missions that deliberately practise different meanings of one word (FR-02), for Personal words that keep the meaning the Learner chose (BR-11, AC-12), and for proficiency that reflects real use. If progress were tracked per Headword, "run" could show as Proficient when the Learner only knows "run a race". The price is a slightly larger data model: every Headword needs at least one Sense, and lookups resolve to a Sense before anything is practised.

## Consequences

- Two Senses of the same Headword can both be Targets in one Mission, because they are different Senses.
- Changing this later means re-keying all progress data, which is why it is recorded here.
